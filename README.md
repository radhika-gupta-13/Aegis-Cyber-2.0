# ⚔️ Overview

**Aegis Cyber 2.0** is a cybersecurity awareness and education platform designed to help users understand cyber threats, prevent attacks, and respond effectively during security incidents.

The platform provides structured guidance and educational resources about modern cyber risks including phishing, malware, ransomware, password attacks, social engineering, and online privacy threats.

> 🛡️ *Awareness is the first line of cyber defense.*

---

# 🌟 Purpose

The goal of Aegis Cyber 2.0 is to make cybersecurity:

* Accessible 📚
* Practical ⚡
* Beginner-friendly 👨‍💻
* Awareness-focused 🔐

The platform is built to spread digital safety awareness and help users adopt safer online practices in their everyday lives.
